Chan-Tat Ng

PhD Candidate in Psychology
National Chengchi University, Taiwan
Advisor: Professor Ting-Ting Chang

How does mathematical thinking develop? What helps us adapt under pressure? How do gesture and speech work together? I use behavioral experiments, functional neuroimaging, and computational modeling to study how children learn about numbers and how emotion shapes learning. With Professor Kawai Chui, I work on how young children combine gesture with speech. Across these projects, I ask what changes as children grow and how context shapes what we observe.

I’m completing my PhD dissertation on the development of numerical cognition. I welcome opportunities to collaborate on developmental cognition, computational modeling, and cognitive neuroscience. I’d be glad to hear from you.

Explore my research

Development in context.

  • Mathematical cognition and learning

    How do children come to understand numbers? I study what changes as they learn to compare quantities and use numbers in arithmetic.

  • Emotion and adaptive cognition

    I study how math anxiety and emotion regulation relate to children’s learning and well-being.

  • Language, gesture, and multimodal communication

    With Professor Kawai Chui, I work on how Mandarin-speaking children combine gesture with speech. Our recent studies include children aged 8–24 months.
