Chan-Tat Ng

PhD Candidate in Psychology
National Chengchi University, Taiwan

My research focuses on the cognitive and neural mechanisms of mathematical cognition and learning, particularly how these processes change across development. I use behavioural experiments, functional neuroimaging, and computational modelling to investigate these questions. My broader interests include emotion, motivation, and adaptive cognition.

Explore my research

Three themes. One developing system.

  • Mathematical cognition and learning

    How numerical representations, arithmetic processes, and their neural foundations develop across childhood and adulthood.

  • Emotion and adaptive cognition

    How emotion, motivation, and evaluative demands relate to cognitive performance, decision-making, and learning.

  • Language, gesture, and multimodal communication

    How language and gesture contribute to the representation and communication of meaning across development.
